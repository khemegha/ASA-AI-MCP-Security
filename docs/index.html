THE NEW ATTACK SURFACE

You gave your AI the keys to the kingdom. Nobody is checking what it opens.

Your firewall sees HTTPS. Your EDR sees a trusted process. Your SIEM sees nothing at all. Meanwhile, an AI agent with shell access, a vector store and your ticketing system is taking instructions from whatever text it reads next.

01 · PROMPT INJECTION

One email can hijack your agent

Hidden instructions in a document, a web page or a log line rewrite your agent's goal mid-task. It still looks like it's working for you.

02 · TOOL ABUSE

Every MCP tool is a loaded weapon

nmap, nuclei, shell, database, file system. Exposed through MCP, a single out-of-scope call turns your pentest agent into a live attack on your own network.

03 · SILENT EXFILTRATION

Your data leaves one token at a time

Hostnames, IPs, credentials and internal topology flow into model context and back out. No alert fires. No log line looks wrong.
